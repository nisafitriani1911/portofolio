# DESIGN.md

Direction supplied by the site owner (answers given in chat). Read with antislop.md as the filter.

- **Identity:** personal portfolio of Nisa Fitriani Novianti. Telecommunication Engineering student, former Samsung technician and PT Gansa Techno staff.
- **Mood:** warm, editorial, personal. Photo and real experience lead; no tech costume.
- **Theme:** light (cream background, near-black ink). Chosen because the content is people and work history, not a developer tool.
- **Palette:** cream `#faf6f2`, deeper cream `#f1e9e2`, ink `#1f1a17`, muted `#6a5f58`, one accent rose `#be185d`. Accent only on the primary button, the hero tagline, and link hover.
- **Typography:** Fraunces (serif) for headings, a reason: warm and editorial, fits the mood. System sans for body. No uppercase tracked labels.
- **Shape:** one small radius (6px for buttons and photo, 8px for cards). No shadows, glow, glass, gradients, or background grid.
- **Dials:** ENERGY 2, RHYTHM 3 (sections change layout), MOTION 1 (hover states only).
- **Content rules:** no invented numbers (skill percentages removed), no emoji, no decorative arrows.
